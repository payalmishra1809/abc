## PAGE 1 · TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE & ROUND 1: TREND TRIVIA

### 1. General Tournament Protocol
- **Eligibility & Squads:** Up to 8 Registered Teams. Each team consists of 2–3 active members.
- **Fair Play Architecture:** Questions and host controls are strictly isolated on the Master Console. Participant mobile devices function exclusively as synchronized millisecond buzzers. No electronic assistance, smartwatches, or AI search tools are permitted.
- **Digital Engine:** Real-time WebSockets handle millisecond buzz ranking, automated scoring calculations, and live scoreboard broadcasting.

---

### 2. Round 1: Trend Trivia (Simultaneous Alphabetical Option Callout)
- **Objective:** Evaluates foundational technical awareness across foundational emerging technology topics.
- **Format:**
  - The Quiz Master projects each question on the main auditorium screen along with 4 distinct choices labeled **A, B, C, and D**.
  - The Quiz Master reads aloud the entire question and all 4 options.
- **Buzzer Rule:**
  - **NO BUZZER is used in this round.** Every team participates simultaneously on every question.
- **Deliberation & Submission:**
  - Once the question reading concludes, teams are given a **15 to 20-second consultation countdown**.
  - Upon the Quiz Master’s cue (*"Options UP!"*), all 8 teams simultaneously display or state their chosen alphabet: **A, B, C, or D**.
  - Once declared, team selections cannot be altered or withdrawn.
- **Correct Answer Reveal & Digital Scoring:**
  - The Quiz Master clicks the central console to visually unveil the correct answer option on the main screen.
  - **Correct Option:** **+10 Points** awarded digitally to every team that correctly called the right alphabet.
  - **Incorrect / Skipped Option:** **0 Points** (Strictly zero negative marking in Round 1).
  - Scores are automatically compiled and reflected instantaneously on the master leaderboard.

## PAGE 2 · ROUND 2: BUZZER BLITZ & ROUND 3: PICTURE THIS

### 3. Round 2: Buzzer Blitz (High-Speed Reaction & Direct Recall)
- **Objective:** Tests lightning-fast recall, tactical reflexes, and strategic risk assessment under negative marking pressure.
- **Format:** Direct technical questions (No multiple-choice options provided).
- **Millisecond Buzzer Protocol:**
  - The digital buzzer on all participant smartphones remains **LOCKED** while the Quiz Master reads the question.
  - As soon as the question is finished, the Quiz Master triggers **"Unlock Buzzer"**. The participant screens illuminate in green.
  - The first team to press their buzzer within milliseconds wins the exclusive right to answer. The Master Console displays the full millisecond queue.
- **Answer Window:** The buzzed team must initiate their verbal answer within **5 seconds**.
- **Scoring & Penalties:**
  - **Correct Answer:** **+10 Points**.
  - **Incorrect Answer / Hesitation:** **-5 Points (Negative Marking)**.
- **Pass / Second Buzz Rule:**
  - If the first team provides an incorrect answer, the Quiz Master re-opens the buzzer for the remaining teams. A successful pass answer scores **+5 Points** (no penalty for an incorrect pass attempt).

---

### 4. Round 3: Picture This (Visual & Media Identification)
- **Objective:** Rapid visual identification of pioneering tech artifacts, corporate founder emblems, humanoid robots, silicon dies, and satellite payloads.
- **Format:** High-resolution visual slide shown on the central stage display.
- **Buzzer Mechanics:** Operates strictly under the **Buzzer Blitz protocol** (Locked until visual is revealed, then unlocked for fastest buzz).
- **Scoring:**
  - **Correct Identification:** **+10 Points**.
  - **Incorrect Identification:** **-5 Points**.

## PAGE 3 · ROUND 4: RAPID FIRE, SCORING MATRIX & DISPUTE PROTOCOL

### 5. Round 4: Rapid Fire (60-Second Timed Solo Sprint)
- **Objective:** High-velocity knowledge endurance testing individual team composure and breadth under time constraints.
- **Format:**
  - Each team takes the floor individually for a dedicated **60-Second Timed Sprint**.
  - The Quiz Master fires up to 10 rapid questions spanning Easy, Medium, and Hard tiers.
- **Passing Rules:**
  - If a team does not know an answer immediately, they may call **"Pass"** to advance to the next question.
  - Skipped questions will not be re-read.
  - The digital countdown timer automatically sounds a buzzer at exactly 0.0 seconds.
- **Scoring:**
  - **+10 Points** for every correct answer delivered within the 60-second window.
  - **0 Points** for incorrect answers or passes (No negative deduction).
  - **Speed Bonus:** An additional **+10 Velocity Bonus Points** is awarded if a team successfully answers 7 or more questions within the 60 seconds.

### 7. Tournament Code of Conduct & Fair Play
1. **Device Integrity:** Participant mobile phones must remain locked onto the participant buzzer interface. Any background switching to browsers, search engines, or messaging applications results in instant forfeiture.
2. **Speaking Protocol:** Only the team member who hit the buzzer (or designated team captain) may deliver the official answer.
3. **Quiz Master's Finality:** In all matters involving acronym expansions, audio clarity, or timing ambiguities, the decision of the Quiz Master is final, absolute, and binding.
